For my whole life I've seen people talking about crypto. Someone lost their life savings. Someone doubled their money overnight. Someone young became a millionaire in hours. People checking their phones every two minutes, pouring hours into it every day. I didn't like any of it. It felt like people chasing shortcuts to money and burning their lives in the same lane, usually without building anything good.

I thought that was all crypto was. Reading the Ethereum whitepaper showed me how wrong I was. What I'd seen was one small, very loud corner of it. The people who built this had something completely different in mind.\
Here's the problem they were trying to solve. Right now, if you want to move money or just keep it safe, a bank sits in the middle. It's centralized. They hold your money, they decide whether your transfer goes through, they can freeze your account, and by printing more money they can quietly shrink what yours is worth. You don't really have another choice. So a man named Satoshi Nakamoto asked a simple question: does money actually need a middleman? And he built Bitcoin as an answer.
