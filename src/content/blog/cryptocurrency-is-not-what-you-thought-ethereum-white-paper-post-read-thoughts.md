**What is cryptocurrency about?** Decentralization. Satoshi Nakamoto - creator of Bitcoin - prioritized principles of transparency, individual sovereignty, and protection against the hidden tax of inflation architecting  decentralized, mathematically bound monetary system. You don't trust some centralized authority - you follow certain rules that's set by systems (called Protocols) as everyone else. It's decentralized because it runs by a distributed network of computers.

**How Bitcoin works?** In its core it's about transactions - you have your own wallet, you have coins, you can transfer it to anyone else's wallet. It's called peer-to-peer. The thing that surprised me the most is the way everything is systemized, designed. Let's say you want to put 1 BTC (bitcoin) into your wallet - you want it to be written in bitcoin's blockchain - the history of all transactions that have been made ever.
